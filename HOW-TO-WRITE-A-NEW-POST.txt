===================================================================
 HOW TO WRITE A NEW POST  —  Bini's blog
===================================================================

You only ever work in ONE folder:  posts-source\
You never touch any HTML. You just write, then double-click build.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 1 — Make a new text file
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Go into the  posts-source\  folder.
Copy an existing file (for example  a-letter-to-my-children.md )
and give the copy a new name, using dashes instead of spaces, e.g.:

    the-day-you-were-born.md

The file name becomes the web address, so keep it simple and lowercase.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 2 — Write your essay
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Open your new .md file in Notepad (right-click > Open with > Notepad).

At the very top, fill in this little block (keep the three dashes):

    ---
    title: The Day You Were Born
    date: October 25, 2026
    excerpt: One or two sentences shown on the home page.
    image: images/posts/the-day-you-were-born.jpg   (optional picture)
    category: Memories                               (optional topic)
    ---

Then just write below it. Normal writing. A blank line starts a new
paragraph. That's all you need.

Optional extras while writing:
  ## A heading            ->  makes a section heading
  > A line like this      ->  makes a nice indented quote
  *a word*                ->  makes that word italic
  — Dad                   ->  a line like this becomes the sign-off

About the two optional lines:
  image:    a picture for the post. Put the picture file in the
            images\posts\ folder first, then point to it here.
            If you skip this line, a default picture is used.
  category: groups the post under a topic. It shows as a little tag
            and appears in the "Browse by topic" menu on the home page.
            (Examples you've used: Letters, Faith, Memories.)

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 ★ STEP 3 — THE PRIVACY CHECK (read this every time) ★
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Your blog is PUBLIC. Anyone with the link can read it, and once
something is online you should treat it as permanent (people can
copy, save, or screenshot it). So before you publish, run this
quick check. You can be as open and heartfelt as you want about
your FEELINGS, FAITH, and LESSONS — just stay private on the FACTS
that could identify or locate your family.

  DO NOT put in a post:
    [ ] Your children's full names, birthdates, ages, or school
    [ ] Photos that clearly show their faces
    [ ] Your home address, street, or neighborhood
    [ ] Your church/daycare name or regular places + times
    [ ] Times the house is empty ("every Tuesday we're all out")
    [ ] Phone numbers or financial details
    [ ] Answers to password questions — first pet, first street,
        mother's maiden name, exact birthdate (these get accounts
        hacked)
    [ ] Other people's names/stories without their okay

  SAFE and good to share:
    [ ] Your lessons, values, faith, reflections, advice
    [ ] Memories told WITHOUT names/places/dates
        ("when you were four, you asked me...")
    [ ] First names or nicknames only — or just "my children"
    [ ] General time ("years ago", "when you were little")
    [ ] Prayers, encouragement, the timeless things

  THREE QUICK QUESTIONS before you hit publish:
    1. Could a stranger use this to find or identify my kids?
       -> if yes, soften it.
    2. Would my child be upset this is public when they're 20?
       -> if maybe, keep it gentler or private.
    3. Is any of this a password-recovery answer?
       -> never publish it.
  If it passes all three, it's safe to share.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 4 — Build the site
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Save your file. Then double-click:  build.bat

A black window opens for a second and closes. Done!
Your new post now exists, and the home page lists it automatically
(newest first).

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 5 — See it, then publish
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Open  index.html  in your browser to read it locally.
When you're happy, upload the whole folder to GitHub (see README.md)
and it goes live at https://binieth.github.io

-------------------------------------------------------------------
 That's the whole system. Write in posts-source, run the privacy
 check, double-click build. You never touch the formatting.
