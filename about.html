About

Hi, I'm Bini.

This is my little corner of the internet — a place where I write things down before I forget them. Some of these are lessons I learned the hard way. Some are mistakes I made and what they taught me. Some are just small memories I never want to lose. I am writing them here, slowly, one at a time.

Most of all, I am writing this for my children. One day, when you are older and I am not around to tell you these things in person, I hope you will read these pages and know me a little better — and maybe find something here that helps you.

It is not a business, and it is not for show. It is my notebook — my thoughts, my faith, my questions, and the small lessons life has handed me along the way.

If you have found your way here too, you are welcome to read. I hope you find something that stays with you.

— Bini
